Krang Uthjook and TeSharani

The great war, and the Fall that ended it. Everything infernal begins here.

What happened

The Holy Princes’ council once had nine members. Two of them, Thamael, the highest ranking, and Haelael, led the opposition to Jangshe’s order in the Krang Uthjook. They lost. The defeat was the Fall: the losing side was cast out of Aempaera and changed, and everything that Ishphaeno is began there. The two took new names, Sadok and Lioshe. Seven Holy Princes remained.

Other peoples were drawn in. Children of Vatrajarə and humans, the Vo’im, already existed, and Aempaera had turned some of them away. That refusal is part of what drove their family toward the war. Many of the fallen’s later children never saw Aempaera at all. The first Lamiae, the first generation, nearly all died in the war, and the Shavari twins fought in it with honour. See The Lamiae and The four broods.

How long the war lasted, and when it happened, is not set down in the current references. Open

What the Fall did to the body

The Fall turned wings into other things, and the fate of each pair depended on which pair it was. Those who had no wrist wings do not grow antlers from that pair, though a few grew antlers anyway.

Antlers and horns

Antlers are living bone with a blood supply, and they grow in velvet before they harden. They come from wrist wings changed in the Fall: corrupted celestial life, still alive and growing. During the velvet phase, the most powerful beings of the infernal side are briefly vulnerable. Horns are fixed keratin over a bone core and have no blood supply. They come from the ankle wings of those born into Toluqavadoth after the Fall. Colour matches the bearer’s own demon skin, with white bone showing beneath, in natural tones only.

A new voice

The fallen carry Jaħuvatra in Vatrahu, Jaxuvatra in Vatraxu, the mirror of the Holy Princes’ True Voice. It is a voice that compels through broken trust, not holy presence. Where Ashraavatra surfaces guilt, Jaħuvatra amplifies desire, despair or compulsion, depending on the wielder’s purpose and the listener’s weakness. The name means “broken voice,” from jaħu, fragmented, and vatra, voice or trust.

What followed

The fallen made Ishphaeno from Toluqavadoth’s body, and Sadok built its capital, Xathqaelsel. Sadok himself is imprisoned in Ashraaħəmavel, the punishment facility inside the crystal desert of TeDrona, beyond the heat zone of Pharuhona. The imprisonment was secured by a deal between both primordials. The Holy Princes’ regiments guard it in rotation. One standard rotation is 60 Earth years, a full human lifetime on purpose. An Ishphaeno year is 2.1 to 2.7 Earth years, so that is about 22 to 28 Ishphaeno years, or about 360 Aempaera years. Uthrijael and Kaprijael alternate on the longest turns.
