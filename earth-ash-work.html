Work and standing

What the House hunts, what it charges, what lives in its territory, and how the rest of the world sees it.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Earth reference documents. Tags mark anything less settled. Nok spellings follow the current reference documents.

The contract function

The House answers cosmic imbalance, and it lets outsiders bring an imbalance to its attention for a price. Rejected petitions get no reply. Silence is the answer.

PaymentThe cost
Years of lifeA share of the petitioner’s remaining life, given freely and for good.
Core memoryA significant memory burned out of the petitioner’s thread entirely.
Oath-bindingA bond to cosmic neutrality for a set time: no supernatural advantage and no contract.
Sacred offeringA soul object, divine relic or cursed item of real weight. The House assesses and does not haggle.
Vow of silenceA lifelong oath never to speak of the House. Breaking it counts as a new imbalance.

A petition that is accepted is confirmed in set words: “We, the Thámaijí’Ójèpa, acknowledge your plea. The weight has been measured. The echo is of [nature of imbalance]. Correction is required. Payment is set. Silence is sworn.”

Secrecy

Members never reveal their affiliation, the House’s existence or their missions, to humans, Sekh, Anu, clergy or any other being, unless a mission puts them in direct conflict, in which case negotiation is allowed. The secrecy feeds the legend, and the House likes it that way.

  • The trigger phrase. Collectors and Witnesses in deep cover each have a personal phrase, known to one Weigher, that cuts through a long-held cover identity and brings back who they are.
  • The owl and the spellbound lips. Witnesses cannot speak outside House places. They send written notes by an owl that is sacred to Nèbémün and trained to cross the Ways. A Collector who receives an owl knows what it means.

Headquarters and dispatch houses

The main headquarters is in the Ways and not fixed to any place. Members find it by feel, nearness to Kàtúngámün and trained recognition, and petitioners never find it. The founding Ashborn’s continuing work holds it steady. It is where members eat, rest, take assignments, deliberate and return when badly hurt.

A few dispatch houses on the living-world side serve as entry points, petition desks, staging grounds and wardrobe stores. Each runs under a long-term cover, and the veil is naturally thin there.

Creatures of the Ways

Zhí- is the Nok prefix for beasts. It marks a creature, not a liminal one, and anything can fall into the Ways, so many kinds turn up there. These are the species the House tracks most.

Oil-slick fur that swallows light, a horse-like head with a six-segment snout that unzips, retractable tentacles, three independent eyes on each side with star-shaped pupils, and a whip tail. They range from large-dog size to house-sized. Weak to blessed light, harmonic disruption, Sekh or Anu bites, the presence of Zhímá’ạt’tárá, or weapons made from their parts.
