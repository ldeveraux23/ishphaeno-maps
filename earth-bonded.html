Bediens and Servi

Humans tied to Lamiae. Some tied themselves, some were caught, and some were born into it.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Earth reference documents. Tags mark anything less settled. Nok spellings follow the current reference documents.

Bediens

A Bedien is a human in a relationship with a Lamiae, willing or not. The range is wide.

  • The addicted. Humans who sought out Lamiae blood for its euphoric, aphrodisiac effect and could not stop. The first effects are intensely pleasurable, and the long-term toll on the body is heavy. Many began as willing and underestimated it.
  • The contracted. Humans who signed a blood contract pledging life force, service or both as security for a deal with a Lamiae master. Some owed a debt, some were taken, and some were born into it, bred across generations in Lamiae households.

Standing

Bediens have almost no legal standing in Emsehurst, and their bondage is invisible to the wider human world. Inside SxSW they are more open, working as performers, domestic workers and, in private, as food and in sexual service. Outside SxSW the bondage is hidden. They appear on no register. Pandora’s Pact protects humans from being forced into this life from the general population, but in practice only the Sekh enforce it, and only while they are present and watching.

Servi

A servus (plural servi) is a step above a Bedien: a human formally blood-bonded to a Lamiae master through a short but permanent ritual. The bond is total.

Gains
Senses and endurance well beyond a normal human: faster, sharper, harder to hurt. Still below a Lamiae or Sekh.
Loses
Autonomy. A servus cannot disobey a direct order from their master. It is a total power exchange written into the body.
Death clause
If the master is killed, the servus dies too. It does not work in reverse, and a master can outlive a servus, though some choose not to.
Where found
Close to their masters, as drivers, bodyguards, household staff and personal assistants.
