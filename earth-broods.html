The four broods

Four mothers, four overlapping pregnancies, four species. Only one of them is the Lamiae.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Lamiae reference (v3). Tags mark anything less settled.

Four women carried children at the same time, and each gave birth to a different people. These are distinct peoples, not four kinds of Lamiae. All four hunted on Earth at first. Most then withdrew into Ishphaeno and spread to the places that suited their bodies, not always staying near their mothers. Decided

That makes 17 original children in all. The Shavari are the only brood unable to reproduce. Decided

Veskari

Serpentine and omnivorous. They have long bodies, flexible spines, powerful joints and limbs, and skulls a little wider and longer than a human’s. Their hands and feet are broad and strong, with gripping pads that let them climb sideways and upside down. A crocodile-like tail grows from the tailbone.

Skin
Tough and a little scaly, in steel blue, sea green, seafoam and swamp green.
Eyes
Pale amber to silver-green irises with vertical pupils.
Tongue
Exceptionally long and versatile. It can reach their own eyes.
Build
Males are generally larger and more imposing.
Habitat
Sensitive to light, so caves and underground waterways suit them.
Hunting
They ambush from ceilings, and can submerge to stalk, seize and drown prey. They are not truly aquatic.

Shavari

Their natural forms differ between the sexes, but both twins are mostly androgynous. The sister leans masculine and the brother feminine. Vatraxu personal names are usually gender-neutral, though some pick up gendered associations in time.

Skin
Ashen purple with pale blue and white star-like flecks.
Eyes
Silver sclera, gold irises and black segmented pupils like a gecko’s, with several pinholes.
Features
Nubian features, a large black 4A afro and slightly sharpened ears.
Horns
Short, straight and upright, like antennae, above the forehead and brows.
Wings and tail
Golden gossamer wings and a thin, wispy black tail that is very expressive.
Body
Fine cat-like fuzz across the midriff and hips, calloused hands and feet with strong nails, full chests and sculpted hips within a graceful frame.

Masquerade. A Shavari body flows naturally between forms. They can imitate another body’s look and proportions without gaining its powers, and they often default to copying each other. They talk without words, through shifts of features, posture, expression and colour. This is not telepathy. Decided

Borrowed power. Eating another species gives a weaker, temporary copy of that being’s inborn supernatural abilities. How strong it is and how long it lasts depends on the one eaten. The store fades with both time and use, with no fixed timer. They remember an appearance after the borrowed power runs out. They need human flesh and are choosy eaters. Decided

The twin bond. Distance dulls their power only a little, but apart, deliberate shifting is harder, and one twin can stay stuck in a form far longer than is natural. Hurting one troubles the other and may upset the shape or stability of either, even side by side. They were trained in restraint, discretion, a steady public identity and guarding each other. They fought with honour in the Krang Uthjook and are remembered by a constellation once linked to “Two Brothers” or “Two Sons.” Decided

Kharuvi

Smaller, slighter, light-boned fliers that eat carrion. Wing surfaces are joined to the arms and unfold like an accordion, as pleated membranes mixed with feather-like structures. The arms help in flapping, and the legs fold to cut drag.

Face
Stubbier, with sharper mouths and small pointed teeth.
Hands and feet
Strong grasping, tearing toes and claws, which they sharpen like a cat.
Skin
Mahogany to ash-white, for camouflage.
Eyes
Yellowish sclera, brown to nearly black irises and round pupils.
Build
Females are generally larger and more aggressive.
Attack
They strike in flight or step forward, cock the head to one side and let out an incapacitating screech.
Weakness
Sensitive to heat.
