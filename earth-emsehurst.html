Emsehurst

A river city that belongs to more than one kind of living thing. It began as two towns facing each other across the Squannock River.

The two towns were never cut off from each other. Roads, trade and above all the ferries of the Porters District joined them long before the interstate. Incorporation in 1924 did not erase the old divisions of culture, housing and money. Decided

How the city began

Hurste, on the eastern bank

Hurste began in what is now the Zingan Quarter, around Moreau College and the Temple, and spread north. Its founders were Sekh and the human Zingans who followed them, looking for a place where no government would reach them. Decided Many Quaker families settled there later. Their belief in the Inner Light and practical tolerance let them live quietly beside things other settlers fled or burned.

Indigenous communities lived in the surrounding country before either town existed. Relations with Hurste were mostly cordial, with trade, truces, understandings and some intermarriage. When forced removal and persecution drove people from their lands, many sought shelter in Hurste. Some joined its population without taking up the Sekhmetian faith. This almanac keeps those peoples and that history deliberately general. Decided

Emerson, on the western bank

In 1805 a settler caravan stopped north of the Emmers River and founded Emerson. Eight families led it: Emmers, Bonchild, Gilbert, Somerhalder, Payne, Malcolm, Jacobsen and Deschamps. Decided

For a few years the town prospered. Then people began to vanish in the dark hours, even after curfews and a militia. The Jacobsen and Deschamps families were attacked and destroyed, and some of their members may have been Turned into Lamiae. The Malcolm family fled. John Emmers, the settlement’s leader, was taken. Settlers returned from the dark with something else behind their eyes, and the fear that followed ended with a surviving teenager burned at the stake. Prayers went unanswered.

What came was a visitor who called himself Itzal, one of many names he has used over the centuries. He offered five of the town’s families a compact: power to protect the settlement in exchange for a lasting bond. Emmers, Bonchild, Gilbert, Somerhalder and Payne signed. They became the local witch families, and Emerson survived. The Witches of Mephis already existed elsewhere. These five did not found the network. They built its power structure in this city. Decided

In 1924 Emerson and Hurste became the city of Emsehurst. The Quaker families of both towns found each other and formed one Meeting, and have been a quiet, knowing presence ever since.

Who runs the city

Emsehurst is run through ordinary municipal government, long dominated by the five witch families. The Emmers family is its leading political dynasty, as long-lived and prominent as the Kennedys. Many Emmers have been mayor. They are usually non-magical relatives, who are less likely to fall to the madness that witchcraft can bring. Most know something of the supernatural world, though how much varies. The family’s civic power and its supernatural power overlap without always sitting in the same person. The Pact, informal agreements and backroom deals run alongside the council chamber. Decided

The five human families

The Quaker Meeting is five families of different ages and origins, and the arrangement has held for about two centuries. They share a theology of tolerance and an inherited understanding of what the city is. They do not talk about it. They ask few questions of their supernatural neighbours, have never taken anything from them and have never been taken from.

The Witches of Mephis in Emsehurst

The Witches of Mephis hold blood contracts with the being the city knew as Itzal, whom witches call Mephis. Each contract carries a price personal to the signer, and each must be renewed every ten generations. The network is global and about two thousand years old. Emsehurst is its showcase chapter, and the most sophisticated the patron has built. The whole story is in The Witches of Mephis.

Places

  • The Squannock River divides the old western and eastern towns. The Zingan Quarter and the Merveilles Valley lie east of it.
  • Mixed territories are Money Street, the University of Emsehurst, the Zingan Quarter and the SxSW entertainment districts.
  • Anu lands are the mountain and forest country east of the Squannock. See Pandora’s Pact.

Where it is

Emsehurst is a fictional city in the American Northeast, and its state is deliberately never named. It has river valleys, hills, bridges and an industrial past, like Western Pennsylvania and Ohio. To the east rise the Merveilles Mountains, the setting’s Appalachian country and home to the Anu. Chicago, New York and Philadelphia exist as places to travel to. About thirty minutes away lies Asheville, which in this world has a beach and ocean access. Decided

What the city says about itself

Emsehurst has the reputation of Salem or Forks. Its legends are familiar to everyone and part of the city’s identity. Some residents believe, some enjoy the stories, and others have never noticed, do not care, or scoff. The Human Coalition feeds this atmosphere, spreading conspiracy theories that blend real incidents with misinformation and invented threats. Decided

Ordinary humans

Humans have no supernatural abilities and the usual weaknesses: illness, injury, death. Sekh blood has a steroid-like effect when it is not used in the Rebirth rite. Lamiae blood is a euphoric, aphrodisiac and highly addictive drug. Both leave a heavy toll on the body once the first effects fade.
