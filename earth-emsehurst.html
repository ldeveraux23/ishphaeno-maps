Emsehurst

A river city that belongs to more than one kind of living thing. It began as two towns facing each other across the Squannock River.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Earth reference documents. Tags mark anything less settled. Nok spellings follow the current reference documents.

How the city began

Emerson, on the western bank

Settlers travelling along the Squannock stopped where a smaller river, which they named the Emerson, joined it. They cleared forest, built homes and prospered for a while. Then people began to vanish in the dark hours, even after curfews and a local militia. Two entire families died in a single night. The only survivor was a teenage girl, whom the townspeople burned at the stake out of fear. Their prayers went unanswered.

What came was a visitor who called himself Itzal, one of many names he has used over the centuries. He offered five of the town’s families a compact: power to protect the settlement in exchange for a lasting bond. Those five families became the Witches of Mephis, and Emerson survived.

Hurste, on the eastern bank

Hurste grew separately, older in some ways, built by families who came from a different direction and settled the land that would become the city’s richest district. Many were Quakers, whose belief in the Inner Light and practical tolerance let them live quietly beside things other settlers fled or burned. When Emerson took in Hurste and the two became the city of Emsehurst, the Quaker families of both towns found each other and formed one Meeting. They have been a quiet, knowing presence ever since.

The five human families

The Quaker Meeting is five families of different ages and origins. They share a theology of tolerance and an inherited understanding of what the city is. They do not talk about it. They ask few questions of their supernatural neighbours, have never taken anything from them and have never been taken from. That arrangement has held for nearly two centuries.

The Witches of Mephis in Emsehurst

The Witches of Mephis hold blood contracts with the being the city knew as Itzal, whom witches call Mephis. Each contract carries a price personal to the signer, and each must be renewed every ten generations. The network is global and about two thousand years old. Emsehurst is its showcase chapter, and the most sophisticated the patron has built. The whole story is in The Witches of Mephis.

Places

  • The Squannock River divides the old western and eastern towns. The Zingan Quarter and the Merveilles Valley lie east of it.
  • Mixed territories are Money Street, the University of Emsehurst, the Zingan Quarter and the SxSW entertainment districts.
  • Anu lands are the mountain and forest country east of the Squannock. See Pandora’s Pact.

Ordinary humans

Humans have no supernatural abilities and the usual weaknesses: illness, injury, death. Sekh blood has a steroid-like effect when it is not used in the Rebirth rite. Lamiae blood is a euphoric, aphrodisiac and highly addictive drug. Both leave a heavy toll on the body once the first effects fade.
