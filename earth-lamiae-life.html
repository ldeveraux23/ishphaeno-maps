Society, weaknesses and relations

No kingdom and no code. A hunger shared by thousands, a few old bonds, and a long list of things that can kill them.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Lamiae reference (v3). Tags mark anything less settled.

Society

  • Structure. There is no central leader. Hashaeqa is the nominal ruler and rarely pays attention, so the Lamiae organise themselves: everyone out for themselves, with gangs and claimed turf. Covens are loose, with little loyalty unless it is forced.
  • The Sireline. Every Lamia is bound to Hashaeqa through their Sireline. A Sire can die with little consequence beyond some mental pain.
  • Generations. Nearly all the first generation died. The turned generations just after them are rumoured dead or hidden, and every generation since has been weaker. Lamiae call the turned “imperfect,” because the human blood that made them is the weak point.
  • Where they live. Underground economies: clubs, dealing, trafficking. Humans may spot a Lamia faster than a Sekh, but Lamiae pass in the underground, with contacts, makeup and glasses to blend in. Much of vampire folklore comes from them.
  • Feeding from each other is no use, except for personal interest.
  • Moral framework. None is shared. The Sekh and the Anu live by the five-level framework, and the Lamiae have none, which is part of why the Anu hunt rabid ones. Proposed

In Emsehurst

  • The 1964 addendum to Pandora’s Pact made the Lamiae a signatory with the same obligations as the other parties.
  • The Pact protects every human in and around Emsehurst. Taking a person from among them by force breaches it, and that includes trafficking. The trafficking that Lamiae criminals do is done in breach of the Pact, not outside it. Decided
  • SxSW is where the Lamiae and their Bediens are most visible.
  • A servus is bound by something a Lamia does, not by feeding. If the master dies, by any cause, the servus dies. See Bediens and Servi.

What hurts a Lamia

WeaknessEffect
SunlightThe day belongs to the Vatrajarə, so the sun is the Lamiae’s enemy. Brief exposure burns, and prolonged exposure burns them to a crisp. They keep a strict night cycle. Protection charms exist, but must be worn at all times and wear out the longer they are in the sun.
FlamesHealing kicks in at first, but it is hungry for energy, so a large or well-fed fire can kill. Some charms protect against the elements.
AethmaaleHoly steel, forged to kill the fallen and their children. It burns outward from the point of contact. Fatal to Lamiae.
SovoqInfernal steel. It can hurt a Lamia, but nowhere near as badly as Aethmaale: a wound that would kill from Aethmaale is survivable from Sovoq. Decided
KraethjarəThe aura flare of a Vatrajarə, in Vatraxu Kraethjaro. Depending on the blast it can burn a Lamia to dust.
Sekh bloodSmall amounts cause vomiting and unconsciousness, and large amounts kill. Recovery takes vomiting or bloodletting and fresh human blood.
StarvationAbout a week, then madness, then a wizened husk.

How others see them

PeopleRelation
The SekhOlder than the Sekh and roughly equal in power. Lamiae blood is toxic to Sekh, and Sekh blood is toxic to Lamiae. Each has made weapons from it.
The AnuThe Anu hunt “reckless or rabid” Lamiae. A large amount of Lamiae blood sends an Anu into a berserker state for 12 hours to 3 days. Strict Anu enclaves keep a quarantine after contact.
HumansPrey, Bediens and Servi.
The Witches of MephisLamiae blood is needed for the stronger witch spells. Witches also supply fortifying spells to Bediens by contract.
The VatrajarəTheir saliva makes the Vatrajarə ill. Their light and their steel are the Lamiae’s worst weaknesses.
HashaeqaNominal ruler, and Sire of every Sireline.
