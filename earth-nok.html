The Nok

The people and language of Nẹránamün’s home. Nok is the liturgical language of the faith, and its calendar and schools are used across the Earth realm.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Earth reference documents. Tags mark anything less settled. Nok spellings follow the current reference documents.

Names

People who speak Nok name children after a common ancestor, a personal trait or achievement, a favourite god, or the name that matches the calendar day of birth. Each day of the first twelve months of the Name Calendar (Khínai’gbasídí) carries two or three names. The thirteenth month is sacred and has none.
