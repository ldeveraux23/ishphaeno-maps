Pandora’s Pact

An Agreement for the Protection of All Peoples Within the Bounds of Emsehurst. Signed in 1933, with a Lamiae addendum in 1964.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Earth reference documents. Tags mark anything less settled. Nok spellings follow the current reference documents.

At a glance

Signed
Emsehurst, 1933. Addendum 1964.
Parties
The Sekh, the Witches of Mephis, and a human witness. After 1964, also the Lamiae.
Not parties
The Anu, who are recognised as allies. Humans in general, who are protected by the Pact but did not sign.
Court and army
None. It is held by the word of those who signed.
Length
It does not expire and needs no renewal.

The preamble says: “We agree to these terms not because we are alike, but because we must share what we cannot divide.” It was made, in its own words, “in necessity, in the middle of a broken world.”

The five articles

ArticleTerms
I. Territory and conductThe Sekh hold the founding lands, the temple district and the Merveilles boundary. Their claim is the oldest and the Pact acknowledges it without granting it. The Witches hold their establishments, family homes and places of practice, and their contracts are their own business. Humans may live, work and move freely without harm or exploitation by any supernatural party. They are not property and not prey, and may not be fed on, bound or coerced without willing and informed consent. No party may wage war, hunt openly or use organised violence in the city against another party or against humans. Disputes go first to the named representatives.
II. The LamiaeNot signatories in 1933. Their presence alone is not a breach. Feeding must be on willing participants of sound mind. Keeping bonded servants is the Lamiae’s own affair, provided nobody is taken by force from the general human population in and around Emsehurst. Organised predation on residents counts as aggression against every signatory.
III. Grey areas and shared spacesMoney Street, the University of Emsehurst, the Zingan Quarter and the SxSW districts are mixed territories. No party may use power, influence or numbers to dominate another’s members there. Grievances go through the representatives. The Anu hold the mountain and forest territories east of the Squannock, and no party may enter without their invitation.
IV. EnforcementEach party answers for its own members, and the offending party’s representative is expected to answer for harm done. The consequence of breaking the Pact is that the peace ends.
V. Duration and renewalThe Pact holds as long as its signatories or their successors uphold it. If a party has no recognised representative in the city, its obligations go dormant, and resume if it returns.

The 1964 addendum

The signatories judged that the Lamiae of Emsehurst had acted in the spirit of the Pact and wished to formalise what had been an unwritten arrangement. The Lamiae became a full signatory with the same standing and duties as those of 1933. Their representative accepted Articles I to V as binding and took personal responsibility for his people’s conduct. The original terms were unchanged.

In practice

  • The Pact’s protection covers every human in and around Emsehurst, not only those inside the city limits.
  • Protection against humans being taken into Bediens status by force depends entirely on the Sekh being present and attentive.
  • The Pact has no way to compel anyone. Its strength is that every party knows what comes after it.
