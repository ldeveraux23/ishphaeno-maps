The pantheon

Nyámün dreamed, and the gods are the dream’s children.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Earth reference documents. Tags mark anything less settled. Nok spellings follow the current reference documents.

The creation, in short

In the beginning only Nyámün drifted in the void. Nyámün dreamed, and Time unfolded as a twin-faced being of mirrors. From Time came Chaos and Order, and from them the Death River, the Veil and the Serpent Guardian. Nyámün went on dreaming and shaped ocean, earth, stars, sun, flame and civilisation. Time’s turning brought wisdom, then Mbòngómün, ancestor of mortals, who was given seven animal spirits. The tellers close with a saying: “Time steps with two feet, yet leaves one path.”

Násítámün and the scarred moon

After the twin moons crashed together, the forces of ebb and flow still raged inside Khùm. Nyámün made Násítámün to be its steward: not the moon itself, but its shepherd, governor and soul. Sekh initiates may pray, “Násítámün, light my hunt. Hold the twin forces in your hands.” A prophecy warns: “When Khùm cracks, Násítámün will weep, and Daimùzónobu will tremble.”
