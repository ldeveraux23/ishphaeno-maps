Schools

Nok education runs from play in the home to apprenticeships and capstone projects. A handful of named academies teach the faith and its fighters.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Earth reference documents. Tags mark anything less settled. Nok spellings follow the current reference documents.

The school year

  • Two main terms, Kwómánèkh (first) and Kwímánèkh (last), with a summer term, Büyémánèkh, between them.
  • Students attend five days of the six-day week.
  • Kwómánèkh runs from ‘Anpémọnai 7 to Lòbárạdímọnai 12. Kwímánèkh runs from Nèbémọnai 1 to Kwọnákhạmọnai 24.
  • Büyémánèkh is six weeks for school students and eight for university students. Older students use it for enrichment camps, capstones and apprenticeships.
  • The long closing break, Kaiméshònítisei, covers Khèmọnai, so everyone is free for the sacred days.
  • Every student takes a one-hour lunch and a mandatory one-hour siesta, in the dorms or a designated rest area.
  • Festival breaks include a week for Tikhètùbẹsé, eight days around Zhawézónímís, and shorter breaks for the Sun, Harvest and Moon festivals.

Yirimbo Zonémi in more detail

Motto: “Zhèmí dạkúmé shù wónírèt,” wisdom begins in wonder. The campus in Hurste, Emsehurst, blends Nubian curves for the Lower School (Yírímbo Zonémimètù) with Hausa geometry for the Upper School (Yírímbo Zonémikhètu), in earth-toned brick and limestone with lattice screens, green roofs, a campus farm, a ceremonial courtyard and a controlled flame basin. It is a private day and boarding school for the children of the Red Choir, Anu-aligned human families, Anu children who have not yet had their first shift, and humans drawn to the sacred liberal arts. It is not for awakened supernatural beings. Sekh aged nineteen or older enrol at Moreau College instead.

Daimushaifimí Military Institute

“Dream Discipline.” Anu-aligned, for warcraft, rescue and supernatural leadership. Outsiders call it Dreamguard or Daimushai, and elders call it Sóróbusù Academy, “Standing Wolf Academy.” Students enter at nine to eleven after a rite of first light. Cadets move through the Daimúni phase (years one to five), then the Shadow Fang phase, with specialisations in healer, hunter, tactician, defender and scout.
