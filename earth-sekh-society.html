Titles and clans

Sekh society is organised by clan, house and years since Rebirth.

Structure

  • Tier 1. ‘Amázíngámün, the head priestess.
  • Tier 2. The Zhèmuno, the Original Clan: the nine founding members, who together serve as her Kaubímùna. Each is also the head of a clan of their own. Two of the nine sit above the rest as her top Bímùna.
  • Tier 3. Individual clans. Each is built from a Zhèmuno member’s initiates and their initiates’ initiates across the centuries.
  • Tier 4. Houses (mùnomù) within clans. A house of good size has its own Bímùna beside its head.
  • Tier 5. Individual Sekh, then acolytes, then ordinary human worshippers.

The nine founding clans

A Zhèmuno member’s middle Nọk name is their clan name, and it becomes the last name of every member of that clan. Zhèmuno itself is the origin clan, so the head priestess has no separate clan name. The oldest and densest clan networks are in the African cradle territories.
