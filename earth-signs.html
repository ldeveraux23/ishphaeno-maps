How sign and alignment work

  • Sign comes from the date of birth and never changes. It is tied to the soul’s imprint through the moon Khùm and the sacred patron.
  • Alignment is Feather (Shùnẹ) or Fang (N’zaikís). It is found by ritual or by quiz, and it reflects how the soul walks the thread in this life. Trauma, awakening or spiritual growth can shift it.
  • Each sign has a Fang aspect and a Feather aspect, and two spirit paths: the Khùzí (born self) and the Khùzú (made self).

When the quiz and the birth sign disagree

A person can be born under one sign and score the Dreamwalker on an alignment quiz. The source notes offer three readings: Proposed

  • A Sign-Bound Dreamwalker: keeps the birth sign but carries dual-thread potential. Exceptionally rare, perhaps born under an eclipse or a Daimùzónobu storm.
  • A misalignment in childhood: the alignment shifted, the sign did not. A Thread Correction Rite is rarely performed.
  • A false reading: the Dream tests us with illusions. The Kihódání‘obuwásóka rite confirms the truth.
