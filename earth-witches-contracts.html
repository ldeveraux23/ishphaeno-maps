Contracts and power

A witch’s magic is a loan with terms. The terms run in the blood, and they come due every ten generations.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Witches of Mephis reference (v3). Tags mark anything less settled.

How a contract works

Power
A blood contract with the patron. The bond weakens over time and must be renewed every ten generations.
Specialty and sacrifice
Each contract has a specialty and a sacrifice, the price. A witch also gains the general ability to see magic and a small natural resistance to its influence.
Coming into power
A full witch comes into power between 8 and 14 or 15. A mixed full witch may take until 18. A sacrifice that shows from birth is always present. A sacrifice that does not show from birth arrives gradually over the year after renewal.
Refusing to renew
The family loses all its power. Depending on the patron’s mood, the family may be wiped out.

The architecture

The patron is Mephithoveles, the first of the eight princes of Ishphaeno. Mephis is the name the witches know him by, and the system of blood contracts is his design. Decided

  • The gift. What he gives a witch is a shard of his own soul, split off and bound into theirs. It is a one-way gift. He stays whole. The witch carries a piece of him for as long as the contract holds, and does not entirely remain themselves, because something of him lives in them from then on.
  • The raw material. His domain is pride, and with it worship: the energy a person gives off when something in them changes because of what they were offered. The contract turns that into power for the witch and a harvest for him.
  • The shape. A family contract is open-ended and runs down the bloodline for as many generations as it renews. There is no set end.

An older line at work: the Drummonds. By the early 1810s the contracts were already old enough to be a trade. In the Scottish Highlands the Drummond line, with its power over barriers and wards, had a fifth-generation witch in Griselda Drummond, who married into the name Hawthorne and was known in society as Lady Hawthorne. Her coven cleaned and sold cursed artifacts to private bidders from her own cellar, among them a Lamia’s agent, a human occultist and a rival witch, and her wards kept the cellar and its guests safe. The Drummond price is the body’s own feeling. Each time a Drummond casts, sensation drains from the skin and the nerves, a little at a time and for good. The more she casts, and the stronger the casting, the faster it goes. By middle age Lady Hawthorne could hold a wide barrier almost in conversation and had not felt pain properly in years, and in company this read as perfect composure. Witches did not hide the arrangement from one another. They ran it as a business under their patron’s terms. Decided

The Drummonds are shown as one example. The terms of other lines are not published here. Each line’s contract is its own. See the ten family lines. Decided

Generations

Original signers are Gen 1, their children Gen 2, and so on. Adoption and marriage adjust this: a half-witch adopted by a Gen 2 becomes Gen 1, and a non-witch adopted becomes Gen 2. On renewal the 10th gen becomes the new Gen 1. Full-witch children alive in that generation join it as their sacrifice matures over the year, and children born within that year are new Gen 1 as well. Children born later are Gen 2.

What each generation can do

The abilities stack, so a Gen 1 witch can do everything below. Decided

GenerationAbilities
Gen 1 to 3Bond minions. With Lamiae or Sekh blood, cast enhancement spells on humans.
Gen 1 to 5Basic barriers and protections, such as warding a house. A spirit or animal familiar, which acts as a messenger and a spy. Spirits cannot physically affect Earth.
Gen 6 to 9A weaker version of the family specialty, and the ability to create blood contracts.
Gen 10Nothing until renewal.

Minions are familiar-like servants and constructs: a raven familiar, a golem, a water witch’s water animus. Decided

Amplifying a spell

  • More than one witch casting in unison.
  • New moons and equinoxes.
  • The essence of a magical creature, such as a Fae or a Lamia.
  • Lamiae blood in particular boosts a spell significantly and, for the strongest spells, may be necessary. Decided

Witches have the usual human weaknesses, a small natural resistance to magic, and a stronger susceptibility to Temptation unless their specialty or an artifact counters it.

Renewal

  • The ceremony. The head of the 10th gen negotiates with the patron in a family-wide ceremony where he is summoned. Only full witches take part.
  • The age. The head negotiates at 19 by default. Some families have special contracts. The Gilbert line is one, and renews when its 10th gen turns 30. Decided
  • The weakest generation. The 10th gen always starts with no abilities, no specialty and no sacrifice paid.
  • The choice. Renew as it is, or offer a new sacrifice for a new power. Renewing with the old sacrifice makes everyone stronger for a year before the older generations fade. A new specialty makes every earlier generation lose its power over six months.
  • Signing. The head signs in their own blood. Today that means a fountain pen loaded with a vial of blood, because many agreements bond with the genetic code. Any witch making a sacrifice that year signs too.

Who leads a line

Each line decides for itself, and the methods differ by culture: the original signer, if alive. A biological hierarchy with a Main Family in charge. A contest of power and will among the 10th gen. Seniority. An election with campaigns and tests. Or a multigenerational council with a 10th-gen chair.

Marriage, half-witches and mixed lines

  • A non-witch spouse may sign the family contract by addendum, make the sacrifice and become a full witch in their spouse’s generation. They may instead stay a non-witch with no access to magic.
  • A half-witch, with one full witch parent, cannot practise magic but always perceives it.
  • A half-witch who marries a full witch may sign on and become a full witch one generation up from their spouse.
  • A half-witch and a full witch have an even chance of a witch child, who can inherit either line’s specialty. A child who does not practise is a mixed half-witch.
  • A half-witch and a non-witch always have a non-witch child.
  • Only mixed full witches can sign or renew contracts. Mixed half-witches must be adopted or married in.
  • Two full witches from different families have a child who manifests one ability with its sacrifice and is counted with that line.

What a half-witch perceives

It is passive and unchosen. It is sensory and impressionistic, never forensic, and it cannot be suppressed or trained into magic without a contract. Many half-witches never attend a Conservatory and invent their own frameworks.

  • True forms. Supernatural beings cannot hide their nature, though the half-witch has no automatic way to name it.
  • Enchanted objects. A faint luminosity, or a sense of wrongness.
  • Active castings. A flash or ripple, with no knowledge of the spell or the caster.
  • Residual magic. A scent, temperature or sound, the same each time for the same person.
  • They cannot name species without education, read signatures, detect a servus bond, or perceive magic that has fully faded.

Line dedication

By default a child takes the line of the gestational parent. A dedication overrides that. In Selo it is Insignatio Lineae. A child can be dedicated to a line at three points: before birth, after quickening and before the seventh month; at adoption; or as an adult, in a harsher ritual with a sponsor. Decided

Both parents attend, with a representative of the chosen line and an officiant who is a contract scholar. Witnesses and an elder of the releasing line are optional.

BeatWhat happens
1. NamingFull names, magus names, lines, generations and prices are declared.
2. The sigilA Selo sigil is drawn on the womb in blood-and-element ink. The default line’s sigil may first be traced faintly, then overwritten.
3. Vow and registrationA record copy goes to the parents and the chosen line, and the patron’s registry is updated.
4. ReleaseThe sacrifice flares. The sigil may vanish, fade, or heal into a scar like a C-section.
  • The carrier feels the chosen line’s sacrifice for a while. The baby has no physical mark.
  • If the gestational parent is human, the rite creates the bond and hits harder.
  • Dedicating a child to a 10th-gen line locks the child into the renewal stakes.

Selo and spells

Selo (Se³lo³) is the universal infernal administrative language and the spine of every official document. Decided

  • It has six tones. A syllable is (C)(w/j)V(N), and the only codas are n and ng.
  • It has four scripts: sin¹ si³, logoglyphs; yu¹ si³, a helper syllabary; ro¹ si³, a romanisation; and do¹ lo³, the native reading.
  • In¹ marks the plural. Aspect and sentence-final particles carry mood, among them zó, mǎ, hà, lâ, lân, nè, mà and má.
  • Latin supplies many roots. IGN-, for example, becomes ing¹.

Spells are cast in the regional Great Tongue, which is Latin in the Americas and Europe, with short Selo “Min” forms and glyph writing. Each circle’s own Great Tongue is used alongside Selo. Decided
