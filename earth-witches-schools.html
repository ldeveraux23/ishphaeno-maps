The Mephis Consortium

Sixty schools in seven circles, joined by one administrative tongue, one entrance test and one rule about how long a student may stay.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Witches of Mephis reference (v3). Tags mark anything less settled.

Core rules

  • Selo is required everywhere. Each circle also teaches one or two regional Great Tongues.
  • Study. The standard course is four years: Foundation in years 1 and 2, then specialisation in years 3 and 4. The maximum enrolment is six years. Years 5 and 6 are extended study: repeating a year, changing tracks, research or teacher preparation. At the end of year 6 a student must graduate, join the staff or apprentice, or be released.
  • Foundation. A unified curriculum of Magic Life Skills (cost literacy, spellcraft, wards, contract literacy, healing, divination and ritual basics) and liberal arts. Nobody skips Foundation. After three years without stabilising, candidacy ends and the student is referred back to line elders or mentors.
  • Leaves and transfers. A leave of absence need not count toward the six years. Transfers within the Network keep a student’s standing, and years add up across schools.
  • Age of entry. 15 to 20 for full enrolment.

Junior programmes and legacy students

  • Junior Programmes take early manifesters from 8 to 15. They do not count as full enrolment and cannot be used to get around the six-year cap. Asian-circle schools more often run residential lower divisions. North American schools may keep early manifesters at home and use day classes.
  • Legacy status is for the contractless: Lineborn 10th-gen heirs, half-witches, adopted wards and declared dependents. They get non-fuelled instruction only (Selo literacy, history, contract law, cost theory, forms, circle layouts, sigils and ward diagrams). They are barred from fuelled casting, high-risk rituals and combat tracks, and their time does not count toward the cap. A 10th-gen heir who signs becomes an Active Witch, and the clock starts.
  • Adult late inductees, over about 22, are trained mainly by line, house or spouse, with modular Conservatory courses. A young spouse inside the 15 to 20 window may be admitted as a full student. A spouse who does not contract may attend select lectures only.

Getting in

  • S-CAT, the Standard Consortium Aptitude Test: a written and oral exam, a practical manifestation check and a confessional interview. It can be retaken once a year after a total crash. It is a placement tool, not a filter.
  • Local trial, Gate 2. It differs by school. In most circles the token lasts one lunar cycle, each witch gets at most two tokens and two full attempts, and trial windows come twice a year, a season apart. A guide leads the candidate to the site once per attempt, and after that the candidate finishes alone. The outcomes are pass, place-down (admitted with stricter pacing) or fail (no admission this attempt).
  • The Consortium Circuit. The competitions: Clause and Contract; Wards and Structures; Applied Casting; Control and Cost; Craft and Interface; sparring; and obstacle events that are not yet named.
