Hashaeqa

The first of the four, and the nearest thing to a queen that Ishphaeno has.

This section follows the Ishphaeno Governance Compendium and the Aempaera references. Lines without a tag are settled. Tags mark anything less settled. Names are given in Vatraxu unless a Vatrahu form is named. It is written to be read before the books, so some family detail is left out on purpose.

At a glance

Title
Jami, one of the four Jamija.
Gate
Naethvatael (Naethva), North quarter. Decided
Species
Vatrajaxo. See The Vatrajaxo.
Character
Ambitious, sensual, possessive, proud and willful. The first Jami and the symbolic matriarch of the four. She oversees Ishphaeno as a de facto queen by the weight of her place in society. It is social and political capital, not a formal title.
Sons
Mephithoveles, Belzevuv and Zamodes, the three eldest of the eight princes. She is also the mother of the first Lamiae. See The Lamiae.

Her quarter

The North: the most politically central quarter and the oldest trading hub, closest to the capital. It holds the port Qaeldreth and the volcanic edge. See Places and Powers.

Dress

She wears her key pin openly, disguised as the most prominent piece in her cascade of asymmetrical chainwork. She does not need concealment. She needs the confidence to let it be seen and to trust that most will not dare touch it. See Clothing and Regalia.

Elsewhere

She is also called the nominal ruler of the Lamiae and the Sire of every Sireline. See The Lamiae.
