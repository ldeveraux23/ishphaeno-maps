Lioshe

The Morning Crown. He governs a kingdom he did not found and has come to love.

This section follows the Ishphaeno Governance Compendium and the Aempaera references. Lines without a tag are settled. Tags mark anything less settled. Names are given in Vatraxu unless a Vatrahu form is named. It is written to be read before the books, so some family detail is left out on purpose.

At a glance

Names
Lioshe after the Fall, Haelael before it. Lioshe Maethluq in full. Scholars of the Abrama tradition know the same figure as Helel. Decided
Family
The younger brother of Sadok and the identical twin of Mikael. The eight princes are his nephews. Decided
Title
Maethlukan, “Morning Crown.” There is only ever one Lioshe. Decided
Before the Fall
Theraapaev, a general of the Holy Princes with wrist wings. He and Sadok led the opposition to Jangshe’s order.
Species
Vatrajaxu. See The Vatrajaxu.
Seat
Xathqaelsel, which is both the capital and its palace, on the Xathhona obsidian plains.
Role
Steward, not sovereign. He keeps Sadok’s institutions until the Twilight Crown returns.

The steward

Lioshe holds nominal legal authority over the whole of Ishphaeno and sets the kingdom’s laws. His authority is real but loosely exercised outside the capital. How closely each prince follows the law inside their own territory is left to that prince’s discretion, much as a provincial governor decides how a central statute is enforced. All eight princes keep rooms and staff in Xathqaelsel and most of them grew up there. It is the place where they are legible under court observation. Their own territories are where they go to escape it. Decided

In public Lioshe is devoted to restoring his brother. In practice he neither obstructs that goal nor advances it, having grown deeply attached over the millennia to the kingdom he keeps. Decided

Haelael on Earth

Before the war Haelael was a general of Aempaera, and gate-travel carried him to Earth. He was drawn by the flare of a gate opening over new life in an alien ley field, where three Hvaena had fled to after the Ambush: Staenhild, Nosmaera and Hjordin. He stayed among them and fathered the children who became the proto-Rejfê. Decided See Rejfê and the timeline.

He was tender with them, and the survivors’ lives were tangled with his. After the Fall that ended. He became Lioshe, stopped visiting, and his children never knew him as a father. They inherited a resonance they could not name. Decided

The bargain with Skandaer

After the Fall the Gate-Maker Skandaer bargained with Lioshe. Lioshe swore to keep the Vorfae hidden for the safety of the survivors on Earth. In return Skandaer helped shape the gate system of Ishphaeno, and taught gate-craft to Lioshe and Sadok directly. Decided

The gates are containment, not a gift. They are strong enough to work and restricted enough not to spill past a bounded depth, so that Ishphaeno stays a caged network and not a cosmic infection. The skill passed to the princes as a birthright. Decided See The Vorfae for Skandaer.

After the Fall

Lioshe’s back wings blackened, like Sadok’s. He has two tails. One is thinner and whiplike, forked with three gripping fingers, and the other is simply forked. The antlers in his regalia are covered on Clothing and Regalia, where the circlet is built around them. What happened to the rest of his wings is not recorded. Open
