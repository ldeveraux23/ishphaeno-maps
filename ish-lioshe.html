Lioshe

The Morning Crown. He governs a kingdom he did not found and has come to love.

This section follows the Ishphaeno Governance Compendium and the Aempaera references. Lines without a tag are settled. Tags mark anything less settled. Names are given in Vatraxu unless a Vatrahu form is named. It is written to be read before the books, so some family detail is left out on purpose.

At a glance

Names
Lioshe after the Fall, Haelael before it. Lioshe Maethluq in full. Scholars of the Abrama tradition know the same figure as Helel. Decided
Family
The younger brother of Sadok and the identical twin of Mikael. The eight princes are his nephews. Decided
Title
Maethlukan, “Morning Crown.” There is only ever one Lioshe. Decided
Before the Fall
Theraapaev, a general of the Holy Princes with wrist wings. He and Sadok led the opposition to Jangshe’s order.
Species
Vatrajaxu. See The Vatrajaxu.
Seat
Xathqaelsel, which is both the capital and its palace, on the Xathhona obsidian plains.
Role
Steward, not sovereign. He keeps Sadok’s institutions until the Twilight Crown returns.
Marriage
Married to the four Jamija after Sadok’s imprisonment. Decided

The steward

Lioshe holds nominal legal authority over the whole of Ishphaeno and sets the kingdom’s laws. His authority is real but loosely exercised outside the capital. How closely each prince follows the law inside their own territory is left to that prince’s discretion, much as a provincial governor decides how a central statute is enforced. All eight princes keep rooms and staff in Xathqaelsel and most of them grew up there. It is the place where they are legible under court observation. Their own territories are where they go to escape it. Decided

In public Lioshe is devoted to restoring his brother. In practice he neither obstructs that goal nor advances it, having grown deeply attached over the millennia to the kingdom he keeps. Decided

How he is known

To most of Ishphaeno’s citizens Lioshe is the only ruler they have known. His name is on every decree, he is rarely seen, and loyalty to him is more habit than love. Decided

The Jamija treat him as king in public. After Sadok’s imprisonment all four were married to him, since Sadok had never wed any of them. Decided See The Jamija.

Haelael on Earth

Before the war Haelael was a general of Aempaera, and gate-travel carried him to Earth. He was drawn by the flare of a gate opening over new life in an alien ley field, where three Hvaena had fled to after the Ambush: Staenhild, Nosmaera and Hjordin. He stayed among them and fathered the children who became the proto-Rejfê. Decided See Rejfê and the timeline.

He was tender with them, and the survivors’ lives were tangled with his. After the Fall that ended. He became Lioshe, stopped visiting, and his children never knew him as a father. They inherited a resonance they could not name. Decided

The bargain with Skandaer

After the Fall the Gate-Maker Skandaer bargained with Lioshe. Lioshe swore two things for the safety of the survivors on Earth: to keep the Vorfae hidden, and to stay a presence on Earth who answers when they call for help. In return Skandaer helped shape the gate system of Ishphaeno and taught gate-craft to both brothers. Decided

Sadok built the gates of Ishphaeno with that craft and founded Xathqaelsel on the ground where he fought Qavasha, the last known Navaxushe. Lioshe worked beside him, and the two are said to have made it together. Decided

The gates are containment, not a gift. They are strong enough to work and restricted enough not to spill past a bounded depth, so that Ishphaeno stays a caged network and not a cosmic infection. The skill passed to the princes as a birthright. Decided See The Vorfae for Skandaer.

The oath and the oathtree

Scholars say the oath was sealed in living wood. Skandaer gave a tooth, and Lioshe wound a lock of his hair around it. The tooth was set in the ground. Then the two stood over it with their forearms crossed and their fists raised, and squeezed their cut palms until the blood of each fell upon the same spot. Sadok and two leaders of the Vorfae of that time are said to have stood witness. Decided

A tree is said to have risen there, in the forests near Hjamblynd. It is called Ndajihvane in the old tongue, the oathtree. It is said to thrive while the oath is kept and to wilt as it fails. It does not hold the gates. The bargain does, and if the bargain fails the gates loosen, the people of Ishphaeno may slip through unchecked, and the bounded depth of the containment gives way. The Lyndfe who remained on Earth are said to tend the tree and to tell the tale of those who guard the world against a great calamity. Decided

For a time Lioshe answered the Vorfae when they called. When he could not answer directly a few times, they stopped calling. Decided

Lioshe stopped visiting his children after the Fall. Some read that silence as part of the oath’s cost, since showing himself too often would have exposed the people he swore to hide. Others say he simply let them go, and that in time they forgot him. Open

After the Fall

Lioshe’s Fall followed the chart for his rank, Theraapaev. His wrist wings became antlers, his ankle wings became his two tails, and his back wings blackened, like Sadok’s. Decided One tail is thinner and whiplike, forked with three gripping fingers, and the other is simply forked. The antlers in his regalia are covered on Clothing and Regalia, where the circlet is built around them. By the same chart his eye wings would have become a second pair of eyes. Proposed
