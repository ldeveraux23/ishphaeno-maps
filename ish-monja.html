The Lamqaxath Monja

The eight princes of Ishphaeno. Each holds a quarter, a capital and an army of their own.

This section follows the Ishphaeno Governance Compendium and the Aempaera references. Lines without a tag are settled. Tags mark anything less settled. Names are given in Vatraxu unless a Vatrahu form is named. It is written to be read before the books, so some family detail is left out on purpose.

At a glance

Name
Lamqaxath Monja, the Eight Princes. Individually each is a Lamqa. Decided
Species
Vatrajaxo, children of the fallen. See The Vatrajaxo.
Court
Xathqaelsel, under Lioshe. All eight keep rooms and staff there.
Gates
The four Jamija hold the gates. Their authority runs parallel to the princes’, not over it.
Dress
The Lamqavael, an eight-fold wrap in full Luthaevel. Eight pleats stand for eight princes. See Clothing and Regalia.
Gates
All eight can open and travel through gates between Ishphaeno and Earth. The skill is a family inheritance from the gate-craft of Skandaer. See Lioshe.
Motto
Veth kol taalun. Open

Each prince has seconds and feudal chains for day-to-day work and commands their own army. Princes hold wide territories, and estates sit within them rather than at the edges. Mamon’s capital is the floating island Phoruvael, with his estate Somavrel on the same island. Korashel, Belishael’s private estate, sits at the eastern corner of Mavetovu. Decided

Velut and dabit

Every prince carries a velut, a passive aura tied to their sin that touches everyone nearby, like weather. The dabit is the velut made deliberate and aimed at one person. A prince can switch each on or off at will and set the strength of each separately. A dabit does not create a feeling from nothing. It deregulates an emotion the person already has. For Lamjamon, Dagan and Zarazael the marking itself is the only step that needs magic. Decided

The same rungs are worn, not only held. A TeMize wears one pleat, a Kharu three, a Veshael six and a Lamqa eight. See Clothing and Regalia.

Held back

The princes sometimes make mortal identities for themselves and live among humans for a time. Those names are not published here. Their other powers and their histories are part of the books.
