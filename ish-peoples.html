Peoples of Ishphaeno

The fallen, their children and the royal house they built from the body of Toluqavadoth.

This section follows the Ishphaeno Governance Compendium and the Aempaera references. Lines without a tag are settled. Tags mark anything less settled. Names are given in Vatraxu unless a Vatrahu form is named. It is written to be read before the books, so some family detail is left out on purpose.

The house

SadokThe Twilight Crown: founder of Ishphaeno, imprisoned in TeDrona.LiosheThe Morning Crown: steward of the kingdom in Sadok’s absence.The Lamqaxath MonjaThe eight princes, their quarters, capitals and estates.The JamijaThe four gate wardens: Hashaeqa, Naevoshi, Pholaeth and Zaelith.

The species

The VatrajaxuThe fallen Vatrajarə, changed by the Fall.The VatrajaxoTheir children, born after the Fall, and the tiers they fill.The Vo’im and the KaemengThe hybrids, and where they stand in both realms.The four broodsThe Lamiae, Veskari, Shavari and Kharuvi.

How the pieces fit

The Fall made two kinds of infernal being. The Vatrajaxu are the Vatrajarə who fell. The Vatrajaxo are their children, born into Toluqavadoth. Sadok and Lioshe are Vatrajaxu. The Jamija, the eight princes and the four broods are Vatrajaxo. Above them all stand the Holy Princes of Aempaera, who fought them and guard the one the war was lost for. Decided

The Shaelruq reigns and the Maethlukan governs. The eight princes hold the quarters, the four Jamija keep the gates, and beneath them the great houses, estate governors and appointed administrators run the kingdom day to day.
