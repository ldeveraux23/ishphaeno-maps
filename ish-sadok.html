Sadok

Founder of Ishphaeno, the Twilight Crown, and the one the war was fought for.

This section follows the Ishphaeno Governance Compendium and the Aempaera references. Lines without a tag are settled. Tags mark anything less settled. Names are given in Vatraxu unless a Vatrahu form is named. It is written to be read before the books, so some family detail is left out on purpose.

At a glance

Names
Sadok after the Fall, and Thamael before it. Scholars of the Abrama tradition know the same figure as Samael. Decided
Brother
Lioshe, his younger brother, who was Haelael before the Fall.
Title
Shaelruq, “Twilight Crown.” The title belongs to Sadok alone. Decided
Before the Fall
Ashraatipaev, the highest of the Holy Princes of Aempaera. He was the only one to hold that rank, with five pairs of wings.
Species
Vatrajaxu. See The Vatrajaxu.
Founded
Ishphaeno, made from the body of Toluqavadoth, and its capital Xathqaelsel. See The Realm.
Now
Imprisoned in Zashraaxomavel, the punishment facility in the crystal desert of TeDrona. See Places and Powers.
Family
Four mates, the Jamija, and the eight princes of the Lamqaxath Monja. The royal house shares the family name Shaelrukan.

The Twilight Crown

Sadok remains the rightful sovereign of Ishphaeno in law and in tradition, though he has not ruled in person since the war. In his absence the kingdom is governed by Lioshe as steward. Over the millennia Sadok has become less a deposed monarch than a founding figure. Many of Ishphaeno’s people now speak of him the way other cultures speak of a divine ancestor, and the regency has quietly encouraged this because it strengthens the dynasty and keeps the kingdom united. Decided

After the Fall

The Fall changed his body as it changed all the fallen’s. His wrist wings became the widest antlers of any being, spreading like an Alaskan moose’s and nearly black. His eye wings became a second pair of eyes and his temple wings a third. His ankle wings became three prehensile forked tails, each tipped with three talon-like fingers that move independently, with fur that matches the black of his wings. His back wings are blackened and he kept them. He carries Jaħuvatra, the broken voice. See Krang Uthjook and TeSharani.

Imprisonment

His imprisonment was secured by a deal between both primordials, Jangshe and Toluqavadoth. The Holy Princes’ regiments guard Zashraaxomavel in rotation. One standard rotation is 60 Earth years, a full human lifetime on purpose. Decided

Dress

Sadok’s own regalia is not published here. The garments of the royal house are on Clothing and Regalia.
