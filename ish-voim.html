The Vo’im and the Kaemeng

The hybrids: neither Vatrajaxo nor human, and ranked by the blood they carry.

This section follows the Ishphaeno Governance Compendium and the Aempaera references. Lines without a tag are settled. Tags mark anything less settled. Names are given in Vatraxu unless a Vatrahu form is named. It is written to be read before the books, so some family detail is left out on purpose.

At a glance

Names
Vo’im, two syllables with an apostrophe between the vowels. Kaemeng, from the older Gemeng. Both names are the same in Vatrahu and Vatraxu. Decided
Vo’im
Children of a Vatrajarə and a human. Before the war they read as tall and clearly not fully human, in the range of 6 feet 5 to 7 feet.
Kaemeng
Children of a Vatrajarə, or of one of the fallen, and a Sekh. Their name is the same in both languages.
Realms
Both hierarchies. See below.

Strength and standing

A Vo’im’s strength scales with the strength of the Vatrajarə parent. A child of a Paev is not a child of a Theraav. Because of this a Vo’im stands in both hierarchies, in Aempaera’s by that parent’s rank and in Ishphaeno’s by where the family’s power lies. Decided

Aempaera turned some Vo’im away before the war. That refusal is part of what drove their family toward it. See Krang Uthjook and TeSharani.

The Kaemeng in Ishphaeno

A Kaemeng is stronger than most of the Lamqa, the eight princes, and has the potential to rank second only to Sadok himself. Court rumour in Ishphaeno holds that only one Kaemeng exists, and that this one is more than a tale. The court does not say what they are or where they are. Decided

Compare the view from Aempaera on The Vatrajarə, where the Kaemeng are measured against the Holy Princes instead.
