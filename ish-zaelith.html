Zaelith

The builder, who values what works over what impresses.

This section follows the Ishphaeno Governance Compendium and the Aempaera references. Lines without a tag are settled. Tags mark anything less settled. Names are given in Vatraxu unless a Vatrahu form is named. It is written to be read before the books, so some family detail is left out on purpose.

At a glance

Title
Jami, one of the four Jamija.
Gate
Jaeshvatael (Jaeshva), West quarter. Decided
Species
Vatrajaxo. See The Vatrajaxo.
Character
Builder, engineer and creator. She values practical accomplishment over political performance.
Sons
Belishael.

Her quarter

The West: floating islands, cliff terrain and mineral wealth, with an ongoing land dispute. See Places and Powers.

Dress

Her key pin is not a pin at all but a small interlocking mechanism, engineered rather than hidden. Finding it does not help unless you also understand how it releases, since it has to be worked correctly rather than simply pulled. See Clothing and Regalia.
