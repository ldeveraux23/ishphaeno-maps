Jangshe

Primordial Order. A being so large that a realm sits inside it.

This section follows the Aempaera and Ishphaeno reference documents. Lines without a tag are settled. Tags mark anything less settled. Names are given in Vatrahu unless a Vatraxu form is named.

Name
Jangshe in both Vatrahu and Vatraxu. The old spelling Jangwe is retired, and the creator was once called Yannei in older documents.
Nature
A primordial: a generally formless cosmic being, and the force of Order.
Body
Aempaera exists within Jangshe. The stars seen from Aempaera are the stars of Earth, seen from another angle.
Avatar
When it prefers to talk with its creations, Jangshe makes a humanoid avatar. Otherwise it is generally formless.
Children
Jangshe created Ashraaha, and with her the Vatrajarə. Jangshe also made Torusheva, the orb that lights Aempaera.
Counterpart
Toluqavadoth, the primordial of Chaos.

Order is not kindness

Jangshe is the primordial of Order, and the references are firm that Order is not the same as benevolence. Jangshe is not necessarily good. It sets the pattern that Aempaera follows, and it is the source of the Vatrajarə’s gold eyes and their markings, the circuitry that shows in light. Its own energy has seasons. See Aempaera for Romatoru and Hevatoru.

Jangshe and Ashraaha

Ashraaha is the mother of the Vatrajarə, but she is not a primordial. Jangshe made her. She is a nurturer and co-creator with queen-level standing, and her place is below Jangshe’s. The Vatrajarə are children of both, made to suit Aempaera’s heavy light and floating terrain. The Ashraat and the Theraav answer to Jangshe and Ashraaha, and only Mikael can also command the Theraav. See The Vatrajarə.

Jangshe and the Fall

Jangshe’s order is what the Krang Uthjook was fought against. After the war, Sadok’s imprisonment was secured by a deal between both primordials, Jangshe and Toluqavadoth. See Krang Uthjook and TeSharani.
