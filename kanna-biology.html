Biology compared

How the Vorfae and their descendants differ, side by side. Scroll sideways on a phone.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Kanna reference documents. Tags mark anything less settled.

What all the peoples share

  • Live birth through the birth canal, babies cry at birth, everyone breastfeeds, and childcare is communal.
  • Hair is naturally coily, types 3a to 4c, mostly black-brown. Blonde, red, green or blue appear from sun, sea or chemical exposure and vary by people.
  • Nails are human-like. The Vorfae commonly shape theirs to V-points.
  • Dual-set people are sacred in every group. “Dual-set” means both reproductive sets are present and describes anatomy only. Words like bisexual are kept for attraction.
  • Intersex people are accepted and unremarkable in every group except the Dorrwfø.
  • Lighter skin and albinism can appear as rare anomalies in any group. They are characteristic of none, and the lightest skin is the most stigmatized among the Vorfae and the Mylzhfã.
  • Builds describe a frame tendency, not a ceiling on body size.
  • Ley sensing is a physical sense in every group. It is strongest in the Vorfae and Lyndfe and weakest in the Rejfê and the Dorrwfø.
