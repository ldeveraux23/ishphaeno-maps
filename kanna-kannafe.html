Kannafê

Four sovereign grove polities on the eastern continent. They are the Lyndfe who went back, and the clearest living form of Grombaer's teaching.

Grove culture

The grove is the community center, and caring for it is worship. Fruit is shared by everyone. The mèla (soul) is understood to circulate through the grove cycle, the clearest surviving form of Grombaer's original teaching of any people. Canon

The four polities

They form one broad ecological and cultural zone, set apart mainly by water. Their political habits follow from their ecology, not the other way round. Canon

PolityPlaceCharacter
NyrstiryndNorth. The coolest and wettest, with deep riverside corridors and the oldest, slowest-growing groves.Preservationist. A grove that takes centuries to replace makes a people conservative: they truly cannot afford a mistake.
VastiryndWest and interior. Harbors and major river mouths.Pragmatic and open to trade, because ships have somewhere to arrive. Will deal with the Rejfê where it pays.
OstiryndEast and south. The largest southeastern territory, fire-edge forest next to hotter, drier land.Diplomatic out of necessity. Their groves burn, so they must negotiate for refuge, seed stock and grafting rights.
HestiryndSouth. A federation of volcanic islands with some mainland coast and fertile ash soils.Agrarian and humanitarian. The first landfall from the southern crossing, so they meet everyone who arrives half-dead from the sea. The most exposed of the four.

Naming

A full name follows this pattern: [Epithet name] by [Birther's name] uta [Home tree] vanje uta [Grove nation]. A name therefore says who you are, who bore you, which sacred tree you belong to, and which nation you come from. Canon

Hajmblynd means “home-grove” (hjam, home, plus lynd, grove). A returning diaspora founding a new Homegrove under that name is no contradiction with the older Earth-side Hajmblynd, because both are simply called what they are. Canon

Faith and body

  • Social and anatomical mix: about 15% dual-set (sacred), 30% sequential hermaphrodite, 55% dimorphic and 20% intersex.
  • Skin is a little narrower than the Lyndfe's range but broader than the original, reflecting a refugee people that interbred with the Rejfê.
