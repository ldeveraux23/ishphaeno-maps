Peoples

Four peoples live on Kanna today. All of them descend from, or evolved alongside, the Vorfae.

How they relate

  • The Vorfae are the ancestors. They were born on Kanna, left in the Exodus, and are no longer there.
  • Both the Rejfê and the Lyndfe lines arose on Earth during the Exodus. At the Return, the Lyndfe who went back to Kanna became the Kannafê.
  • The Mylzhfã and the Dorrwfø are not part of that split. They evolved independently on Kanna during the long absence, and are closer relatives of the Rejfê than of the Lyndfe.
  • Think of it the way you would think of two human peoples: both descend from the Vorfae, and neither is a subset of the other.

The Fjarafê, who stayed on Earth

The Lyndfe and Rejfê who remained on Earth after the Return interbred with each other and with humans, and became the Fjarafê. They have dissolved into the general Earth population and hold no land on Kanna. They survive as the root of the fae myth, and as the ancestors of the Earth cultures that appear in the Earth section. Naming

  • The peoples' names carry an ê. In the daughter languages the Stamfaen “ae” reduces to ê, so Rejfê, Kannafê and Fjarafê each end in it. The Vorfae keep the full spelling.
  • The Dorrwfø are Dorufê to outsiders, and the Mylzhfã are Muldfê.
