The Vorfae

The ancestral people of Kanna, born of the life-spirit and the tree-spirit, and the source of every culture on this page.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Kanna reference documents. Tags mark anything less settled.

Before worlds: Hvaengur

Before worlds there was Hvaengur. Not a god of nature but life itself: soil, breath, growth, decay and memory in matter. Hvaengur is not a person or a ruler, does not speak or judge, and allows. Four conscious emanations arose from it. Canon

EmanationDomain
Hvandaer, the ShepherdGuide and protector of living peoples, concerned with continuity, balance and survival. Loves Kanna's forests and seasons, but cannot create alone.
Skandaer, the Gate-MakerGuardian of thresholds, paths and limits. Maker and regulator of the Skandaer Gates. Neutral and exacting.
Grombaer, the Past-GrowerKeeper of memory, decay, burial and transformation. Treats death as return, not ending.
Fjarnzaer, the Sister-WorldA world-spirit of distance, exile and elsewhere. Later linked with Earth.

Kresyva and the birth of the Vorfae

  • Hvandaer loved Kanna but had no equal to share that love with. Hvaengur let a partner emerge from the trees themselves: Kresyva, the Tree-Mother, masculine in form but able to bear children, and an equal to Hvandaer, neither servant nor ruler.
  • From their union came the Vorfae. Sex combinations at birth are fluid, and intersex births are common and sacred. They attune naturally to ley lines, can shape technology without cutting themselves off from nature, and are long-lived and cyclical rather than immortal.
  • They spread across Kanna into communes, groves, cities and finally advanced societies.

Grombaer's teaching

The dead return to the soil. From their burial grow plants, the plants bear fruit, and eating the fruit returns memory, strength and belonging. This is the root of sacred groves, ancestor continuity and communal identity. Death is circulation, not loss. It is also literal: every death releases the ley a body carried back into the ambient flow, and every birth draws from it. That is why the rite appears in every surviving descendant people. Canon

The Skandaer Gates

As the Vorfae advanced, Skandaer revealed the Gates: passages between worlds, usable only when the twin moons align, dangerous and limited. They were meant for preservation, not conquest. The Hvaena, the most skilled among the Vorfae at willed-construct work, were trusted to attempt gate travel during the Calamity, since construct mastery was the qualifying skill for the role. Canon

Age-bands

Age shows as band-like tattoo markings that begin at the wrists and climb. A thin ring forms every 10 years. Every 100 years a connecting mark ties the last ten rings together, a century bound. Canon

Vine-SpineA thin vine running upward, touching each of the ten ringsRoots connecting seasons; memory braidedGrove communities
Knot-SealA small knot at the outer forearm with stitches to each ringOath completed; identity declaredCourts, clans, hierarchy
Gate-StrikeA clean vertical line crossing the ten rings like a post through fence slatsCrossing survived; century earnedTravelers, scouts, way-folk
  • The Kannafê keep fewer bands, with longer gaps between them.
  • The Fjarafê usually grow a single band, because so much human interbreeding diluted it. Purer individuals can reach two to five.
  • The Rejfê have none at all, the most ritually diluted of any descendant people.

Ley and magic

  • Every Vorfae-descended people can sense the ambient ley field to some degree. The strength of that sense sets a subconscious base tier of magic (frost, light, fortune, wind, trap): personal and temperament-driven, not assigned by tribe.
  • A rarer elevated tier, willed constructs, belongs only to dual-set individuals in every descendant people. That matches a belief shared everywhere that having both reproductive sets is sacred.
  • The system runs on conservation of energy. Nothing is created, only borrowed and returned.

“Dual-set” means both reproductive sets are present. It describes anatomy only. Words like bisexual are kept for who someone is attracted to. Canon

The Crisis and Kresyva's sacrifice

Through dreams and omens Hvaengur warned Hvandaer, who warned the Vorfae. Skandaer opened paths, but not for everyone. To buy time, Kresyva sacrificed themself, holding Kanna steady long enough for the convoys to escape. Hvandaer and Grombaer stayed behind to preserve Kanna and prepare it for return. Kanna did not die. It slept, scarred. Canon The science behind it is on The Calamity.

The core truth, and how they lived

  • In the Vorfae's own words: life is not created once. It is guided, returned and allowed to continue. They carried that faith to Earth, into exile, and eventually back to Kanna.
  • Kinship is a strong web. Cousins are treated like siblings, and marriage and mixing are fluid and not especially taboo in small communities or among elites.
  • Death is an honor, and when population is low a necessity. The Vorfae live long and are not eager to die, so the honor is heavy, not casual.
