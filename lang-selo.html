Selo

The witch tongue, the language of Mephis administration, taught in every school of the Consortium.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Kanna reference documents. Tags mark anything less settled.

At a glance

PropertyDetail
Also calledWitch-tongue
Where it is usedEvery school of the Witches of Mephis. A text written in Selo is official, and a text only in a regional tongue may be a copy.
VocabularyBuilt on Latin seeds, reshaped to Selo sounds. Latin aqua, water, becomes kwa.
SyllablesEvery syllable fits the pattern (C)(w or y)V(N), with an optional nasal closing it
TonesTonal. Tones are written as numerals or accent marks.

Sounds

GroupSounds
Stopsp, t, k. The voiced b, d and g appear between vowels.
Affricatech
Fricativesf, s, z, sh
Nasalsm, n, and ng at the end of a syllable
Liquids and glidesl, r (a tap), w, y
Vowelsi, e, a, o, u, and a short ə in unstressed particles. There is no vowel length.

Tone

Tone does grammatical work in Selo. Elevating a noun to a formal register means giving it tone 1, so se¹ is “star” in the elevated register. Polite particles all carry tone 1, a neutral root such as lo³, speak, carries tone 3, and commands in the examples carry tone 6. The full tone stack is not summarized here. Open
