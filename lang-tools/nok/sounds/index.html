Dialect

Pick a sound. Click any letter on the charts to open its card: how to make it, a sound you may know from a real language, links to hear it, a place to record yourself and compare, and syllable pairs to practise.

Link-outs go to Seeing Speech (University of Glasgow). Reference audio is a recording made for this site (in the audio folder) or a Wikimedia Commons file. Example words follow the Wikipedia Help:IPA key (CC BY-SA). Your own recordings of sounds that don't exist in real languages are the reference for those sounds. Compared sounds from real languages are approximate: listen to a recording before trusting the match.
