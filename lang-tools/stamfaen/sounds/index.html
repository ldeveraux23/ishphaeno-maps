Pick a sound. Click any letter on the charts to open its card: how to make it, a sound you may know from a real language, a reference recording, a place to record yourself and compare, and syllable pairs to practise.

Link-outs go to Seeing Speech (University of Glasgow). Reference audio is a Wikimedia Commons file or a recording made for this site; each sound uses the same recording in every language. Example words follow the Wikipedia Help:IPA key (CC BY-SA). Sounds with no reference recording are yours to record with the Record button. Compared sounds from real languages are approximate: listen before trusting the match. Cards marked “Needs your ruling” are places where your notes disagree.
