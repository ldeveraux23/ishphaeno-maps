Cards come straight from the almanac page. Urhmauuri has two spellings, the formal one with real IPA letters (ʉ, ʁ) and the keyboard one (uu, rh). Typed answers accept either, and ignore capitals and apostrophe styles. Drafts, such as the calendar and clock words, may still change.
