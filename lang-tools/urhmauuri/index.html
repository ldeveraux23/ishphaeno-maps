Urhmauuri

The language of the Urhmaiia. Open syllables only (CV, VCV, CVV, V), with uvulars, affricates, a palatal nasal and, in the Haulorai register, clicks.

Sound Cards

Clickable IPA charts. Each sound has how to make it, sounds you may know from real languages, a reference recording where one exists, and a place to record yourself and compare.

Flashcards

Vocabulary decks from the dictionary and phrasebook, with flip and typing practice and a special-letter keyboard.
