Vatrahu and Vatraxu

Vatrahu is the language of Aempaera. Vatraxu is what it became among the fallen.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Kanna reference documents. Tags mark anything less settled.

The two languages

Vatrahu is the speech of the Vatrajarə. Its name means the voice or language of trust (vatra is trust or voice and -hu is language). Vatraxu grew from it in Ishphaeno, among the fallen and their children, and it is the working language of the Infernal realm. Names in the almanac are given in Vatrahu unless a Vatraxu form is named.

The four choruses of the Vatrajarə are Ashraat (first), Theraav (second), Paev (third) and Laer (fourth). Mikael carries the title Uthaartraal, from uthjaar, leader, and traal, prince. See The Holy Princes.
