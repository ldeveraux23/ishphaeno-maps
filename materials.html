Materials and Craft

What Ishphaeno’s people wear, build, forge and trade in. Almost every fibre, hide and ornament comes from creatures and plants already in the living things pages.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Ishphaeno garments and material culture reference. Tags mark anything less settled. Clothing is on its own page, and so is music.

Clothing and RegaliaDraping, the rank wraps, the ring and the circlet.Instruments and MusicSkull drums, wing-bone harps, crystal chimes and the three court ensembles.

The logic of cloth

Fur and hide are common, because nearly anyone can obtain and work them. Flawless woven cloth takes generations of specialised craft, and that is what makes it the true luxury marker, not the rarity of the raw material. A Lamqa in impeccably woven fabric displays civilisation itself. A frontier soldier in beautifully worked Ruqorith hide displays competence, not poverty. Neither reads as lesser. They are different registers of one value system: mastery over mere possession.

Hides, feathers, shell and crystal

MaterialSourceWhat it is used for
Ruqorith hideThe Ruqorith herds, which range across every quarter.Thick armour leather, buttery ceremonial leather, suede and embossed leather. Horn doubles as an ivory substitute. The backbone of practical leatherwork. See Ruqorith.
Xathelun furThe tiger-wolf apex predator of Xathdrubooshle.Dangerous to obtain, so ownership is a status marker, like tiger or snow leopard fur. In practice restricted to the Lamqa, the Veshaelja and military honours.
Miravun shellScutes naturally shed by the flying turtles that nest beneath the inverted mountains.Layered breastplates, hair ornaments, combs and ceremonial shoulder guards. A southern luxury tied to Belishael and Zarazael’s territory.
Vorunith feathersFlight feathers shed naturally by the large flying predator that nests atop Mamon’s floating islands.Fans, capes, collars, headdresses and ceremonial standards. Collected without harming the animal.
Qorazil quillsThe hollow-quill mice and their rattling spines.Worked into living fringe rather than sewn flat, so walking makes the garment softly chime. Some families tune their quills on purpose. Others cultivate silence.
Mresorak elytraWing covers of the cattle-sized beetles of the fungal forest.Belt plates, pauldrons, fans and decorative scale-work. Iridescent armour that is not metal. Contested, because Mresibooshle is unclaimed borderland.
SobraevaelFlexible tendrils from the living crystal formations of TeDrona.Braided into belts, jewellery, corset frames and collars. It keeps growing slowly after it is cut, so the jewellery is, in a small way, still alive.

Sobi and sovoq

Sobi is crystallised emotion, and its states run from raw crystal through ore, powder, shaped currency, glass and liquid to forged steel. Metal is precious on Ishphaeno, and sovoq is the unique metal.

Metals

  • Copper (Ruqoluth) and iron exist naturally in lesser amounts, scarce and patchy. Copper is a working metal sold by weight, for wire, fittings and cookware, and it is valued below the currency tiers. Copper-stained rock in the volcanic west is a source. Decided
  • Iron comes from ironsand (magnetite), bog iron in the swamps and red volcanic soils. Its Vatraxu name is left for the naming session. Decided
  • Smiths mirror Mafa smith techniques from the Mandara Mountains of Cameroon. Decided
  • In practice that means a tall down-draft furnace fed with magnetite ironsand, smelting hot enough to make carbon-rich cast iron, a second hearth that burns the carbon off with blown air to make workable steel, then hand forging. Proposed
  • There is no native gold or silver. They exist only as imports from Earth, and they are exotic luxuries and trade curiosities, not currency. Decided
  • There is no tin, so no bronze, and titanium is left out. Proposed
  • A possible clad blade has an iron core with a sovoq edge. Proposed
  • Fire-gifted Vatrajaxu and Vatrajaxo are skilled artisans, not a labour pool. Decided

Salt

  • Salt is sacred. It is a flavouring, a ritual ingredient and an everyday implement. Decided
  • It purifies transitions: union ceremonies such as marriage, contract and christening, and funeral or ending rites. Decided
  • Salt is mixed into the road composition. Decided
  • Vamihona’s salt flats form by evaporation of Xatheloru water on the dry side of the mountains, and Tovuvami is the city of salt. Canon
  • Contracts may be sealed with salt. Proposed

Glass

  • Glass is raw and impure, milky or greenish, never perfectly refined. Decided
  • Sobimael windows stay, but they are raw and tinted by emotion. Decided
  • Slag glass and glass frit may go into the road mix and glazes. Proposed

Sand, stone and ground

  • TeDrona’s sand is bone-white and granular, with coloured veins by emotion type, and some dunes sing or squeak. Bodies left there slowly turn to sand. Decided
  • Qaelvaelvo’s black beach is volcanic glass and basalt sand with a magnetite fraction at the waterline and a little ground sobi. Decided
  • Clay for roads may come from weathered volcanic ash. Proposed
  • Compasses are very unreliable and get worse toward the centre. Decided
  • Soils by region are on the soil map.
