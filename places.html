Places and Powers

The four quarters, their gate keepers, and the cities and estates of the eight princes. Positions are on the soil map.

This page lists places only. Who the princes are, what they are called among mortals and how they relate to one another is not published here.

The four quarters

QuarterGateKeeperPrincesCharacter
NorthNaethvataelHashaeqaMephithoveles, LamjamonThe most politically central quarter and the oldest trading hub, closest to the capital.
EastHaelvataelNaevoshiBelzevuv, DaganWater-rich, the shipbuilding capital, with misty cave systems.
WestJaeshvataelZaelithZamodes, MamonFloating islands, cliff terrain and mineral wealth, with an ongoing land dispute.
SouthTaepvataelPholaethBelishael, ZarazaelInverted mountains, salt flats, the heat zone and the judgment facility boundary.

Each gate is held by one of the four original succubi. They are gate wardens and regional administrators, not commanders over the princes. Their authority runs parallel to the princes’ and their estates sit near their gates.

Princes hold wide territories, and estates sit within them rather than at the edges. Every prince has seconds and feudal chains for day-to-day work, and commands their own army.
