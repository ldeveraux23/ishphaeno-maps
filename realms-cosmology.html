Cosmology and timeline

The two families of myth and power that share one universe, and the order in which their great events happened.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the reference documents for each realm. Tags mark anything less settled. Numbers worked out from canon are marked as such.

Two cosmological families

The universe runs on two separate families of myth and power. They are not meant to interfere with each other. Decided

Earth is where they meet. The Sekh and the Anu follow the Dream family’s framework, and the Lamiae and the witches follow the other. The war in Aempaera and the Exodus from Kanna are separate events, and the timeline below keeps them in one list so you can see which came first. On the Dream side, see Kanna and the faith. On the other side, see Aempaera, Jangshe and Toluqavadoth.

The order of events

Order is firmer than dates. Where no date is given, the sequence is what is settled.

The first age on Earth

No dates are set for these events. The order is what counts. Decided

OrderWhat happensSee
1The Hvaena make a first foothold at Khematin, near the Kush and Nile corridor. Humans fear the newcomers and their children, and the fear becomes a planned strike, the Ambush. The Hvaena flee east along the road later called Ostiraej.Peoples
2The proto-Rejfê are born to the three Hvaena during the Exodus, and the proto-Lyndfe with them. Haelael, a general of Aempaera who has been drawn to Earth by the gate-flare, fathers the proto-Rejfê by all three.Rejfê
3Hjamblynd (Hajmblynd in Kannafên), the “home-grove,” is founded in the secluded highlands near the Cameroon fault line. It is claimed with Grombaer’s rite, which attunes the people to Earth’s ley field. The grove communities that attune this way become the Lyndfe. The Kannafê later name their Kanna Homegrove Hajmblynd after it.Kannafê
4The war in Aempaera, Krang Uthjook, is fought and lost, and the Fall follows. The losing side is cast out and changed, and Thamael and Haelael, brothers, take the names Sadok and Lioshe. Haelael stops visiting his children on Earth. Nearly all of the first generation of Lamiae die in it.The war
5Sadok clears Ishphaeno from the body of Toluqavadoth, with Xathqaelsel as its centre. Skandaer bargains with Lioshe: Lioshe swears to keep the Vorfae hidden, and Skandaer helps shape Ishphaeno’s gates as a contained system.Ishphaeno
6The Return. Word reaches the Earth-descended peoples that Kanna still lives, renewed. Most Rejfê go back, most Lyndfe stay, and a small minority of each cross the other way. Those who go become the four Rejfê tribes and the Kannafê.Rejfê
7The Great Flood strikes Earth, and it hits those left behind. Some die. The Lyndfe and Rejfê who stay on land interbreed with humans and become the Fjarafê, whose descendants are the Nok.Peoples

Nok tradition keeps a distorted memory of the Calamity. Lyndfe settlers arrived on a world with one moon, carrying the memory of a sky with two, and they built a myth to explain the difference: the crashing of Khùzú and Khùzí into Khùm. The Nok call the event Dímakhùm Daimùzónobu. See the faith.
