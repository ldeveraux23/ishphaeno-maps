What you can read right now

A figure is whole when every star shows, partial when the haze or the dark hides some, and hidden when none shows. Pick one to see it on the chart.

How the chart works

  • The band lights the stars it passes. Where it is tells you the hour, loosely, because it wanders.
  • Haze hides stars. It is thickest in the east and thinnest over the dry south, so figures there read whole more often.
  • The stars never rise or set. The figures are a map, not a clock.
  • Each figure has an anchor star, ringed in gold, that gives a bearing. The Sobravrek also has the Rivals, a dashed-ring pair, as a second anchor. Gold dots on the two heroes are rank marks.
  • Dark hanging spires are rare and high. A few hang in the southwest. Their drips evaporate into a faint trail that drifts east, shown as a lighter streak on the haze layer and stronger as you raise the haze slider.
