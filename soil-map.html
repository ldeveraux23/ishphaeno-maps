What changed, and what to check

  • Ruqothona is now an east-west band of red-rock country directly south of the volcanic mountains, with Ruqoravael below it. In your sketch the band does not reach Pojabooshle, so the Geography doc's line about it bordering Pojabooshle needs editing.
  • Triangles in the northwest mark the volcanic mountains (Ruqorejahi). Their count and positions are guesses.
  • Vamihona now stretches far enough to hold all four mountains and their lakes, with Pharuhona and the forest swamp trimmed to match.
  • Every city and prince estate from your docs is now a dot. Éné confirmed the city and estate positions and the beach lines on 17 Oct 2026. Hover or tap a dot to read about it.
  • The northwest peak and Sheluphol shifted left, and Xaelphol's peak and river end moved closer to them. Hevaphol's peak moved up and to the right, and Malophol's peak moved slightly left.
  • Petrified trees now fill a rough valley down the middle of Pojabooshle, and Sheluphol sits at the base of the northwest peak like the other lakes.
  • The floating islands now sit over Ruqoravael and the top of Pojabooshle, not the cliff country. The cliffs have the mines and waterfalls, and their runoff drains into Pojabooshle.
  • Ruqorivael, the green highland, replaces the old forest-to-savanna edge and overlaps both the Ruqothona band and Ruqoravael. Ruqoravael is now drawn as savanna with grass tufts.
  • Qoruvrin is now a loose ring of five around a whirlpool, outside the sea's northeast corner and touching it.
  • The cliff country soil is confirmed: shallow stony ground over bare rock, with deeper soil only in talus piles and terraces.
  • Earlier changes still stand: four base lakes, rivers from the sea with a linking tributary, Malzevrona's channels, runoff and waterfalls in the West, Xathdruvex stands, the farmland belt, and the three shores.
  • Still open: the Vaethpojahi count and which peak holds which lake.
