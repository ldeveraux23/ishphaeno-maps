Toluqavadoth

Primordial Chaos. Every realm of the infernal side lies inside its body.

This section follows the Aempaera and Ishphaeno reference documents. Lines without a tag are settled. Tags mark anything less settled. Names are given in Vatrahu unless a Vatraxu form is named.

Name
Toluqavadoth in Vatraxu, Toluk’avadoth in Vatrahu. The older forms Torqavadoth and Torqavel are retired. The root toluq means chaos.
Nature
A primordial: a generally formless cosmic being, and the force of Chaos. It contains every possibility. It gives off the same atmospheric gases as Earth.
Body
Ishphaeno exists within it. Each realm technically lies in its body. Beyond the borders of Ishphaeno lies the unmapped, ungoverned wilderness of Toluqavadoth itself.
Temper
Neither good nor evil. It takes a quiet pride that so much civilisation, complexity and suffering lives inside it.
Counterpart
Jangshe, the primordial of Order.

What it does to Ishphaeno

SignWhat it is
The tidesThe tides of the Xatheloru sea are Toluqavadoth’s breathing. They are used for coastal navigation, reading its mood and omens, and never for telling time. A suddenly irregular tide means something is disturbing its rest. In the Qoruvrak season it breathes harder, with storm surges and flooding.
Toluqavel, the auroraIts inner state, made visible as shifting coloured light across the false sky. There are six seasons marked by colour, in a most-common order that holds about 85 percent of the time. Because this is Chaos, the order shifts and season lengths vary within ranges. Almanac scholars and diviners can only predict by probability. The most feared case is Qoruvrak twice in close succession, which is said to have happened at least once in recorded history.
Ways inUnlike tightly controlled Aempaera, Ishphaeno has many ways in: gates, cracks, bargains, death and accident.

The false sky, the aurora seasons and the tides are covered on the sky map and in The Realm. The realm itself was cleared from Toluqavadoth’s body by Sadok, with Xathqaelsel as ground zero.
