Creatures and plants

Every creature is two or more real animals fused, with one trait pushed past what nature allows. They skew toward Pacific, Indian Ocean and Southern African coastal fauna.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Urhmaiia Canon Reference. Tags mark anything less settled. Terms are given in the keyboard romanisation, with the uvular letter written rh.

The source animals are real, so this page is a good place to see where each design started. Names are Urhmauuri.

Danger and dependence

Venomous animals such as the Spine Walker and the Spined Puffer can kill careless Urhmaiia, especially children. The exception is Urhmate melded with the same species, who carry resistance or immunity. They are central to antivenom work in every city, which gives them value and also treats their bodies as a medical resource. That tension is unresolved.

Companions and mounts

Dolphins are companions and never food. The Quickcurrent is a thrill-mount, the Willing Partner is a chosen collaborator, and the Elder Pod is a revered friend. The Sterling is kept by some as a companion, which is respectful to some and arrogant to others. Opinion splits by caste and generation.

Materials

Almost every creature above feeds a craft: sealant from hagfish slime, sinew and leather from eel, thread from the noble pen shell, armour from isopod and crab shell, magnetic scales for iron, grown glass for building and light. Open A full materials page is planned.
