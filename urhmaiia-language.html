Language and time

Urhmauuri is built for mouths that vary widely. Its rules are tendencies, not walls.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Urhmaiia Canon Reference. Tags mark anything less settled. Terms are given in the keyboard romanisation, with the uvular letter written rh.

Sounds

Allowed syllables are CV, VCV, CVV and V. The only consonant allowed at the end of a syllable is rh, a sacred exception used in roots such as Urh, “water.” Consonant clusters survive only as an archaism in ancient sacred Haulorai. Vowel harmony is a tendency, and all diphthongs are legal. The rules exist to accommodate the wide range of Urhmaiia mouths.

The letters w, v and q do not exist. Words borrowed with w, v or bh become sh (in front-vowel words) or ny (in back-vowel words), so tuva becomes tunya. The sound lh marks deep native vocabulary, and in loanwords it appears only beside a uvular or after a back vowel.

Politeness

Five grammatically marked levels, set by the main or final verb and kept through the sentence:

RegisterVerb endingUsed for
Plain polite-oMost formal or polite situations.
InformalnoneFriends and equals.
Intimate-eA life partner only.
Respectful-iSomeone higher in status or age.
Royal-uThose who hold royal titles only.

This is separate from the register system for names of Urhaha: Urhahaureiara (formal), Urhaiiza (everyday), Naturhzaia (intimate), Urhzakoa (casual).

The cord family

Urhmaiia have no pockets or shelves, so they use cords:

CordUse
NaialiniThe identity cord. It starts as a carved name-piece and collects meaningful objects over a lifetime. Sacred, worn across the chest and never removed.
AmailiniThe sleep anchor.
ToriliniThe everyday carrying cord for tools, knives and coin strings. Small valuables may instead be braided into hair on long swims.
