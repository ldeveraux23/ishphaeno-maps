Urhmaiia

A long-lived deep-ocean people who live along Earth’s trenches, in a sacred ocean that human science has never mapped.

Lines without a tag are settled canon from the Urhmaiia Canon Reference. Tags mark anything less settled. Terms are given in the keyboard romanisation, with the uvular letter written rh.

At a glance

Names
Urhmaiia is the outsider’s name. Urhmauua is the endonym used in ceremony and oral record. Their language is Urhmauuri.
Castes
Three: Urhmauuka (water-born), Urhmate (water-made) and Urhmake (called back).
Creator
Urhaha, with a sacred current called Ruua running through all water.
Lifespan
Long but not endless. The body begins to fail around 200 years, and few live past it. Deep-trench Urhmauuka may reach about 250.
Homeland
Maubuʁ, the sacred ocean of the deepest trenches, and the “fringe” cities around it.
How they look
Melanin first. Scales, fins, glow and iridescence sit on top of a dark skin baseline.
Humans
Called gizo in everyday speech. Garomaia, “root-people,” is the formal and mythic word.
